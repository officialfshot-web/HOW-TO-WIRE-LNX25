# LN-25X Field Guide

**Open it:** https://how-to-wire-lnx25.vercel.app

<img src="icons/qr.png" alt="QR code for the LN-25X Field Guide" width="160">

Scan the code with your phone camera to open it.

An interactive guide to the Lincoln LN-25X wire feeder, built for welders who know stick or TIG and are new to wire.

## Put it on your phone

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link and tap **Install** in the bar at the top, or use the menu and pick **Add to Home screen**.

Once you've opened it once, it works offline, so it still loads on job sites with no signal.

## What's inside

- **Suitcase:** every part of the feeder (front, back, inside) with plain-English explanations, a "follow the wire" tour, quizzes, and a 2-step / 4-step trigger simulator.
- **Gun:** every part of the MIG gun, connector to nozzle.
- **Load wire:** a hands-on simulation of loading a spool, step by step.
- **Gun technique:** push vs. drag, gun angle, stickout, and weave patterns.
- **Dial it in:** a settings simulator with a "what if" grid and arc sounds.
- **Heat chart:** starting amps and wire speed by metal thickness and wire size, plus memory tricks.
- **Troubleshoot** and a **pre-weld checklist**.

## Notes

Settings are teaching starting points. Always go by the spool label, your WPS, and a test bead on scrap.

It's a static site (`index.html` plus a manifest, service worker, and icons) with no build step. Vercel redeploys automatically on every push to `main`.
